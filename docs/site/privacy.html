Shade Toggle privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-28. Applies to Shade Toggle 0.1.0.

Shade Toggle changes website appearance locally and remembers one dark-mode choice per exact hostname. It has no account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or payment. It does not transmit, sell, or share website content or browsing information.

When you enable it, its bundled rendering engine reads page structure, text/style properties and style sheets to calculate dark colors in your browser. Page content is processed locally, not retained by Shade Toggle. Engine network transport is disabled; inaccessible cross-origin style sheets may limit rendering. The websites you visit continue their own normal network activity under their own policies.

The extension stores enabled hostnames and associated preferences in Chrome's local extension storage, not Chrome sync. It checks the selected tab's URL to identify its hostname and protected pages. Site grants are managed by Chrome. Turning a site off removes its enabled preference and rendering; Chrome's site grant may remain until you revoke it through extension site-access settings. Uninstalling removes the extension's local storage through Chrome. Actual uninstall cleanup remains a manual release verification item.

Permissions: activeTab identifies the selected site following your action; scripting injects and removes the bundled renderer; storage remembers choices; optional site access lets a granted site retain its choice on subsequent loads. Access is requested when enabling a site, not granted for all sites at installation.

The GitHub Pages information site uses no project analytics, cookies or external fonts. GitHub may process standard hosting request information under its own privacy statement.

Questions or requests: use the project's GitHub issues. Avoid posting private page contents. For sensitive reports use the route in SECURITY.md. Policy changes will be dated here and recorded in the changelog.

Public policy URL: https://qwertypants.github.io/chrome-simple-dark-mode/privacy.html. Verified live on October 28, 2026.
